# Agora

Agora is the companion web app for the GenAI Days event (Tuesday, November 17, 2026, Nantes).

**Live app**: https://genai-days-companion.lovable.app

## Features

### Networking

- Sign in with your email, without a password, then complete a short onboarding: identity, photo, activity and professional tags, intentions.
- Participant directory with search and filters (keywords, tags, intentions, AI level, company size, sector).
- Participant pages, with Speaker, Sponsor and Organizer badges.
- Send, accept or decline connection requests.

### Messaging

- Private conversations between connected participants, with real-time delivery, replies, reactions, edits, deletions, read receipts and typing indicators.
- Unread counters and email digests for missed messages.
- Optional push notifications.

### Profile

- Edit your profile with automatic saving.
- Control your account and data.

### Program

- The program, speakers and sponsors are loaded from the event website, [genai-days-nantes.com](https://genai-days-nantes.com), so the app always shows the latest published schedule.
- Chronological schedule of all sessions, with the tracks clearly distinguished.
- Automatic scroll to the session happening now or next on the day of the event, with a "Now" marker.
- Session pages with title, description, format, track, time, duration, room and floor, themes and speakers.
- Filter by track.
- Like sessions and review them in a "My sessions" filter. A warning appears when two liked sessions overlap.
- Add a session to your calendar (.ics file or Google Calendar).
- Rate sessions after they take place.

### Speakers and sponsors

- Speaker list and detail pages with photo, role, company, bio, career highlights, links and the sessions they host.
- Sponsor list and detail pages.

### Floor plan

- Zoomable plans of the ground floor and the 1st floor.

### Everywhere

- Installable on your phone's home screen, designed for mobile first.
- The program, speakers and floor plan stay available offline.
- Available in French and English.
- Accessible, warm and editorial design in the GenAI Days visual identity.
